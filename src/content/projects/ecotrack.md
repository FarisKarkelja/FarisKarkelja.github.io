## The idea

Traffic is one of the biggest sources of air pollution in our cities, but the data that could help - vehicle categories, emissions, air quality readings - lives in different places. EcoTrack brings it together: it connects vehicle registrations and emission categories with air quality data, and uses Google's Gemini model to generate readable pollution reports.

## My role

I organized the team of three for the Google Cloud AI Startup Factory hackathon and worked on the frontend in Next.js and TypeScript, connected to a Node.js and Express backend, Firestore and Gemini, deployed on Google Cloud Run.

## What I learned

Shipping something working in hackathon time means agreeing on scope fast, splitting work cleanly between people, and cutting features before cutting quality.
