## The client

COLAK Hausmeister is a property maintenance company in Germany. Their customers - property managers, landlords and homeowners - want to know quickly what the company does and how to reach it, mostly from a phone.

## What I built

A clean, German-language website that answers those two questions fast:

- An overview of the services [provjeri: npr. caretaking, cleaning, garden and winter service, small repairs]
- A short introduction to the company and the area it covers
- Contact details and a contact form, one tap away on mobile

## How I worked

I owned the whole project: requirements with the client, the design in Figma, a hand-coded build with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Bootstrap, testing on phones, tablets and desktops, and launch. I also set up the domain, SSL, hosting and email, and I maintain the site after launch.

## Result

A professional online presence the client can send to any customer, built to load fast and read well on any device.
