## The client

Autopflege Rottweil is a car detailing studio in Rottweil, Germany. Like most local businesses, it gets customers through search and word of mouth, so the website has one job: show the quality of the work and make it easy to get in touch.

## How I worked

1. **Requirements** - a short conversation to understand the business, its customers and what the site has to achieve.
2. **Design** - layout and visual direction in Figma, agreed with the client before any code.
3. **Build** - hand-coded HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with Bootstrap where it helps. No page builder, so the site stays fast and easy to maintain.
4. **Testing** - checks on phones, tablets and desktops, in different browsers, before and after launch.
5. **Launch and hosting** - domain, DNS, SSL and hosting set up and managed by me.
6. **Maintenance** - content updates and fixes after launch.

## Why it matters

Freelancing means owning the whole software development life cycle with a real stakeholder on the other side: unclear requirements, feedback rounds, deadlines, and a product that has to keep working after it ships. This project is one of four client websites I have live today - the others are listed under Freelance on the projects page.
