## What it is

Unwind is a calm place to decide what to watch. The home page shows what is trending and new, with trailers, and the library is your personal hub: every title lives in Watched or Watchlist, you can search your collection and add or remove titles in one click. Suggestions mix new content with what is already in your library.

## How I built it

I designed the interface in Figma first - the layout, the orange-on-black visual identity and the poster-first library - and then built it during my remote internship at IDK Studio: HTML, CSS and JavaScript on the frontend, PHP and MySQL behind it, with movie artwork from TMDb. The layout is fully responsive, so the library works just as well on a phone.

## What I learned

Designing first made the build faster: most UI decisions were already made, so development was about data and behaviour instead of pixel pushing.
