## What it is

Before I built websites for clients, I built one for myself. The site presents my dance career: a biography, a list of projects and performances from 2019 onward with their year, organizers and collaborators, a gallery of professional photos, and an Inquiry section where people can sign up for a dance class or contact me.

## How it is built

HTML, SCSS and JavaScript with jQuery, with content loaded through AJAX and a light and dark theme.

## Why it matters

This is where I learned the basics I now use every day - responsive layouts, navigation, image-heavy pages and forms. It later became the starting point for the React version.
