## What it is

Gric helps you decide where to eat - or what to cook. It recommends restaurants and recipes, and lets users sign in with Google to keep their preferences.

## How it is built

A native Kotlin app with a Jetpack Compose UI and an MVVM architecture. Hilt handles dependency injection, Room on SQLite stores data locally, Retrofit talks to remote APIs, and Firebase handles authentication with Google sign-in. I spent a full semester on it, so it grew into a large, structured codebase rather than a demo.
